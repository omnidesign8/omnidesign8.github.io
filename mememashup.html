Meme Mashup

My first Meme Mashup.

"America, Take My Little Hand" is a meme mashup that combines Donald Trump with Hanson from Scary Movie 2. In the movie scene, Hanson tries to help someone hanging from a window by offering his little hand, stating it was stronger. I replaced Hanson’s face with Trump and made the person being rescued “America.” The joke comes from combining Hanson’s famous little hand scene with the jokes surrounding Trump’s hands. I wanted the image to be funny, but also say something about the state of society. America is literally hanging off the side of a building, while Trump is offering his “little hand” to save it.
