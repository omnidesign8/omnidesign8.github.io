Glitch Art

My first Glitch art.

This series is called The M00n. This series of Glitch art were experimental, using a few different techniques and applications. For data bending Note Pad ++ was used in which section of code were removed and replaced with the words The Moon. As for Data Moshing I used Photoshop and audacity to create this glitch, here an echo effect was applied. The series exposes the viewer to see the moon not as a fixed celestial object, but as something constantly shifting through memory, technology, and perception.
